Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

The short version

Your data stays on your device. We don't have servers. We don't collect, store, or sell anything.

What Discernment accesses

  • Sent emails — Discernment reads your sent Gmail emails via the Gmail API to learn your writing style. This data is processed locally in your browser and stored in Chrome's local storage on your device.
  • Compose windows — Discernment reads text you're actively writing in Gmail, Google Docs, Notion, and Slack to provide real-time feedback. This text is analyzed on-demand and not stored.
  • API key — Your Anthropic API key is stored locally in Chrome's storage to make API calls for writing analysis. It is never transmitted anywhere other than directly to Anthropic's API.

What Discernment does NOT do

  • We do not operate servers or databases
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data remotely
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize any user data
  • We do not send, modify, or delete your emails
  • We do not track your browsing activity
  • We do not use analytics or tracking scripts

Third-party services

Discernment sends your writing samples to the Anthropic API (api.anthropic.com) for analysis using your own API key. This is a direct call from your browser to Anthropic's servers. Anthropic's privacy policy governs how they handle API requests: anthropic.com/privacy

Data storage

All data — your writing samples, taste profile, fingerprint, and API key — is stored exclusively in Chrome's local storage on your device. You can delete all data at any time by clicking "Reset Everything" in the extension popup or by uninstalling the extension.
